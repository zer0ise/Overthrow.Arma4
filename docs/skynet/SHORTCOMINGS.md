Status: OPEN until a commit on `skynet` closes it (note the hash).

| 15 | Wanted | Wanted is a decaying timer (GTA III rules) that only ticks down out of sight. Want it persistent, with workarounds that lower it (clothes, barber, concealment, hiding, paying someone off). Needs a persistence check: unknown whether wanted state is saved today | Design | `OVT_PlayerWantedComponent` (decay block, `SetWantedLevel`), `Persistence/` | OPEN |
| 16 | Progression | XP/levels are a personal RPG track (level = 1 + 0.1*sqrt(xp); XP from occupier kills +5, trades, jobs, building). Design idea: repurpose as organisational FAME that survives character death, and attracts backers who grant perks (reskin XP to fame, skills to backer perks). Backer perks wanted: discounts, gear, recruitment, maybe reinforcements. Of the 3 skill trees (Trade, Stealth, Diplomacy) Trade and Diplomacy fit; Stealth is personal and stays OUT of the reskin. Gear perk = UNLOCK better kit tiers (shop/import access), not free armour; or recruits/players spawn better equipped. Trade already carries unlocks via permissions (Import, IllegalImports); equipped recruits already exist (tent action buys a recruit wearing a saved loadout, cash + kit price + fee) so a backer could discount or free that. Recruitment and reinforcements are not skill effects today (recruit is cash + cap; High Command files exist, unread). Needs check: what happens to the XP record on death, and where skill points/perks are spent | Design | `OVT_PlayerData.xp`, `OVT_SkillManagerComponent`, `Configs/Player/overthrowSkills.conf` | OPEN |
| 17 | Spawn kit | Starting kit is NOT tied to officer. It is: civilian clothes from `Configs/Civilians/CivilianClothes.conf` + the difficulty's `startingItems` on first spawn only (Easy/Normal: M9, 15rnd mag, R148 radio; Extreme: radio only; startingCash 500 on Easy, 0 on Extreme). Same for every player and class. Wanted: choose a role/class at start and get role-appropriate kit (officer is just a flag with no kit). | Source + play | `OVT_SpawnLogic.OnCharacterCreated`, `Configs/Difficulty/*.conf`, `Configs/Civilians/CivilianClothes.conf` | OPEN |
| 18 | Roles | Officer is auto-granted: always in single-player, to the host in a listen server, to any admin, or if the player id is in the config `officers` list. No choice, no cost, no role selection. Officer gates FOB deploy, real estate, production sites, storage, options, officer loadout templates | Source + play | `OVT_OverthrowGameMode` (OnPlayerRoleChange, connect, init), `OVT_ResistanceFactionManager.AddOfficer`, `OVT_OverthrowConfigComponent.officers` | OPEN |
| 19 | Loot | No searchable furniture. World furniture has no contents. Only one Overthrow furniture prefab (`OVT_CabinetMetal_01_grey_V1`) carries the stash component, and that is a player stockpile, not loot. Looting is the vanilla `SCR_LootAction` (modded only to raise wanted when seen). Wanted: search drawers/wardrobes/crates in the world for items. Constraint: scripts cannot add components at runtime (the storage component's own comments say prefab authoring is the only seam), so each furniture prefab needs a Workbench prefab override plus a loot-filler script | Play + source | `Prefabs/Props/Furniture/`, `OVT_StorageComponent`, `UserActions/Modded/SCR_LootAction.c` | OPEN |
